Simple Nigerian Style for the Modern Man

BIODUN STYLE GRID (BSG) is a premier Nigerian men's native-wear fashion brand founded on the principles of precision tailoring, uncompromised quality, and contemporary elegance.

Our brand voice combines professionalism and friendliness to prioritize exceptional customer service. Each garment—from our signature Senator suits to our crisp Kaftans and regal Agbadas—is crafted to embody modern style while honoring authentic West African craftsmanship.
