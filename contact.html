SHIPPING & RETURNS INFORMATION

Frequently asked customer care details

Lagos express delivery takes 1 - 2 business days. Nationwide delivery across other states in Nigeria takes 3 - 5 business days via express courier. Free shipping applies on orders above ₦150,000.
