How to Measure Yourself
1. Chest Measurement

Wrap the tape measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping tape level under your arms.

2. Shoulder Width

Measure horizontally across your upper back from the outer edge of one shoulder bone to the other.

3. Waist Measurement

Measure around your natural waistline where your native trousers sit comfortably.

4. Top & Trouser Length

Measure from the top of your neck shoulder seam down to your preferred knee or ankle length.
